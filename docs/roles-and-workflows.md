# Roles And Workflows

The system is designed around hospital staff responsibilities.

## Staff Roles

- Admin users manage system-wide information and reporting.
- Receptionists support patient intake and registration.
- Doctors create visits, prescriptions, and clinical charges.
- Nurses support patient care workflow updates.
- Lab staff process lab-related bill items.
- Pharmacy staff handle prescription-related activity.
- Billing staff manage invoices, payments, and disputes.

## Workflow Notes

Billing items can move through different statuses depending on the responsible
department. Clear role boundaries help each dashboard show the work most relevant
to that staff member.
